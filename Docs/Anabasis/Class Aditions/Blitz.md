the blitz is a class about dashing and being mobile. It revolves around it's dashes that allow you to stay near bosses and give I-frames to help with this.
**Daggers** -- Daggers are short range weapons, they can be stuck out to deal damage with left click or can do a dash that doesn't deal damage with right click
**Gauntlet** -- Gauntlets are for dashes that deal damage, these deal less damage then daggers but keep you safer
# Weapons
## Starter
### Wooden Thorn
Dagger; Deals 7 damage, 5 sec cooldown for dash, 4 length dash
Crafted with 20 wood at a workbench
### Copper Gauntlet
Gauntlet; Deals 5 damage, 7.5 sec cooldown for dash, 5 length dash
Crafted with 15 copper bars at an anvil
### Tin Gauntlet
Gauntlet; Deals 5 damage, 7.5 sec cooldown for dash, 5 length dash
Crafted with 15 tin bars at an anvil
## Pre-Boss
### Steel Dagger
Dagger; Deals 18 damage, 2 sec cooldown for dash, 7 length dash
Crafted with 20 platinum bars
### Gem Knuckles
Gauntlet; Deals 7 damage, 5 sec cooldown for dash, 10 length dash
Crafted with 5 of any gem and 15 gold/platinum bars at an anvil
## Pre-Evil Boss
### Antlion Knife
Dagger; Deals 20 damage, 45 sec cooldown for dash, 12 length dash
