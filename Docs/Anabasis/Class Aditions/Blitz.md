the blitz is a class about dashing and being mobile. It revolves around it's dashes that allow you to stay near enemies and give I-frames to help with this.
**Daggers** -- Daggers are short range weapons, they can be stuck out to deal damage with left click or can do a dash that doesn't deal damage with right click
**Gauntlet** -- Gauntlets are for dashes that deal damage, these deal less damage then daggers but keep you safer
