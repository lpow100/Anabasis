The alchemist is a class about brews of items, he turns materials into new ideas and uses these in battles
He is made to have a lot of medium range consumable items
## Weapons
### Pre-Boss

#### Poisoned Stick

Early game wand of sparking type weapon

Crafted with 20 wood at a graveyard biome

Deals 3 damage

Shoots a projectile that inflicts poisoned debuff

#### Broken Bottle

Consumable projectile weapons

1 is crafted with a bottle and 1 stone at an anvil

Deals 10 damage

Shoot a glass shard projectile that deals the damage

  

#### Explosive Vial

Grenade-like consumable weapon. Explodes on impact with anything.

3 are crafted with 5 iron/lead ore, a bomb and a bottle at a bottle

Deals 16 damage in an AOE

Shoots the vial

#### Blood Syringe

Medium damage consumable weapon. 

Inflicts custom Poked (3 dps) debuff, deals 16 damage

4 are crafted with 12 iron bars and 2 lesser potions of healing at an Bottles

#### Stinger Pole

Medium-Low damage consumable weapon

Inflicts poison on hit and deals 12 damage

5 are crafted with 1 platinum bar, 1 jungle spore, and 1 stinger

### Pre-Evil

#### Flaming Crimsrod

Medium damage weapon

Inflicts on fire on hit and deals 16 damage

Crafted with 13 Crimtane bars, 5 chains and 150 Torches 

#### Flaming Corruptor

Medium damage weapon

Inflicts on fire on hit and deals 16 damage

Crafted with 13 Demonite bars, 5 chains and 150 Torches 

#### Painful Pocket Sand

Medium damage consumable weapon

Deals 23 damage

6 are crafted with 15 sand, 1 antlion mandible, and 10 Sturdy fossils
