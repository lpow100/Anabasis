A mod about an ascent to the stars, because the moon isn't the only celestial body.
Anabasis is a greek word that means a going or marching up, in this mod it represents an ascent, going beyond the stars and summoning the worst creatures of space, for a battle. 
## Major Additions
The [[Alchemist]] is a new class added for this mod, he is not directly connected with the idea of Anabasis.
Other additions include:
- [[Bosses]]
- [[Materials]]
- and other [[Changes]]
Post-ML content is the central idea behind this mod, while there are things added in Pre-HM and HM they are not central to this mod and are to balance the game and/or to add to the idea of Anabasis with additions like the Giant Harpy to the sky islands.
## Use with other mods
This mod is not intended for use with other major mods like calamity, though this may be fixed with the use of unofficial or official compatibility mods.
Without compatibility mods I believe it will not work well with calamity though it may play nicer with other major mods.
