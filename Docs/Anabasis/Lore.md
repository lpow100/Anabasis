Back when the solar system was being formed, as planets and moons were coming close to their modern form avatars began to appear. These avatars acted as smaller images of said celestial body and could roam other celestial bodies.

Enough fighting allowed the solar system to fall into a fragile balance where each planet and their moons had enough reason to not fight each other, and the sun finally manifested it's avatar as a response to keep the peace.

Eventually the terrarian kills the golem temple full of warped martians and brings the martians down to earth, starting the collapse of the solar system.

When the moon was summoned by cultists to help restore balance, the Terrarian slayed the moon causing a solar war, as the moon shattered across the stars. As earth loses its ability to communicate in the solar system due to the affects of the Terrarian, the earth lets loose ever harder enemies to vanquish the player

Once the moons of mars notice the carnage, they come down to protect mars due to the murder of its people but end up getting slain, with phobos crahsing down to earth and deimos flying within 100 m of neptune.

As the planets across the solar system discuss the slaughter of the moons, they decide on neptune coming down to earth to return it to an ice age and assume the earth has rebelled due to its lack of communication.

Neptunes avatar lands down on earth in the snow biome and begins to imbue its cold into the snow, as the player uses the superheated versions of minerals found in the crash of phobos to force a fight with neptune.
