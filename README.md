# Anabasis
Anabasis is a content mod for Terraria that adds new content throughout the game on a similar sense to Thorium and Macrocosm. The mod is centered around adding content to bring the avatars of the solar system to life with mostly Post-ML content.

## Current features
- Gauntlets as a new melee subclass found occasionally throughout the game.
- The Giant Eel segmented boss and custom boss bar.
- Phobos and Deimos as the avatars of their respective moons.
- New materials, equipment, tools, accessories, tiles, buffs, and projectiles.
- Planned bosses, progression, and the Basalt Crags biome.

Anabasis is an active work in progress. Some content is implemented, while other features remain documented plans.

## Documentation

Design notes and the current development checklist are available in [`Docs/Anabasis`](Docs/Anabasis). The class and progression documents describe the intended direction of the mod.
